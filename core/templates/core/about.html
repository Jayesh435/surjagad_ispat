Strategic Vision

Gadchiroli: Emerging as the Steel Hub of India

The Surjagad Ispat integrated steel plant in Gadchiroli, Maharashtra represents a significant industrial initiative in line with the Aatma-Nirbhar Bharat vision — building a self-reliant India. Following our Honorable Prime Minister Narendra Modi's statement that "Gadchiroli" will emerge as the "Steel Hub," we have taken the initial step towards realizing this vision.

Our Mission

Building India's Steel Future at Gadchiroli

Surjagad Ispat Pvt Ltd operates an integrated steel plant with an initial capacity of 0.75 MTPA, scaling to 2 MTPA, specializing in Thermo Mechanically Treated (TMT) wire rods. With a ₹10,000 crore investment, this facility aims to boost the local economy through job creation while supporting India's self-reliance in steel production.
